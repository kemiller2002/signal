## Original requirements and acceptance criteria from GitHub issue #125

## Finding
Signal's survey and admin pages import Forma 0.6.1 correctly at the stylesheet level but the static HTML has no deliberate `data-ef-brand` or `data-ef-theme` assignment. This alone is not a styling bug: the inherited default may be correct. The gap is that a **product-approved palette decision** is not evident, and [the October 13 conformance report](https://github.com/kemiller2002/signal/blob/main/docs/verification/ui-conformance-2026-10-13.md) describes strong behavioral/layout testing while recording an unstyled administration-navigation list and other residual presentation debt.

## Requirements
- **SIGNAL-VIS-001.1:** Make an explicit, reviewable decision whether Signal inherits the Echelon default palette or chooses a supported Forma theme/brand (e.g. assess blue/cyan vs bronze, without prejudging). Define intended light/dark and user-preference behavior, deployment defaults and rationale.
- **SIGNAL-VIS-001.2:** Govern hierarchy and semantic color usage across assessment, survey, scoring/results, administrator console, authoring, lifecycle, export/report and visualization: reserved danger/warning, answered/selected, pending, immutable/finalized, errors and permissions must mean the same thing everywhere.
- **SIGNAL-VIS-001.3:** Replace visually unstyled or ambiguous administration navigation with a coherent Forma-composed treatment without contradicting accessibility and layout contracts or secretly restyling Forma internals.
- **SIGNAL-VIS-001.4:** Preserve graph/chart meaning without hue alone using labels, data position and patterns where applicable; test actual populated, empty, ambiguous, failed and lifecycle-transition states.
- **SIGNAL-VIS-001.5:** Produce representative screenshots, computed-color/contrast records and independent hierarchy review in light/dark at narrow/wide viewports, 200% zoom, focus, forced-colors, grayscale/CVD screening. Use real browser + Limen/F# composed path.
- **SIGNAL-VIS-001.6:** Add regression fixtures and consume new Forma token validity, Dokimos verification and Praxis completion checks once available. Preserve the current layout/interaction conformance suite rather than replacing it.

## Acceptance criteria
- A durable product visual-decision record names the approved theme/brand policy, relevant requirements, source/version and any approved exceptions; choosing the default must itself be intentional.
- No unsupported Forma tokens or unreviewed colors; rendered contrast and semantic-state checks pass with evidence.
- Independent design review verifies hierarchy and suitability; contrast alone is not visual-quality acceptance.
- Both respondent and administrator flows, including non-happy states, have explicit test coverage and UNKNOWN is not treated as PASS.
