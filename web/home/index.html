Surveys and assessments

Surveys whose answers live in the link

Signal runs surveys and assessments from published, immutable templates. Each respondent's answers are carried in their own link, not stored on a server, and results are calculated the same way every time.

Answering a survey?

Use the link your survey administrator sent you. Signal never asks you to sign in to answer.

Your answers stay in that link, so you can stop and come back to it later.

Who it is for

Three roles, one fixed meaning

  • Survey authors

    Publish a template whose questions, branching, validation and scoring cannot change after publication.

  • Respondents

    Answer from a link. The link always holds their current answers, so they can stop and continue later.

  • Administrators

    Import completed submissions and read results together with how they were calculated.

How it works

From template to traceable result

  1. Publish a template

    An author publishes a template. Its version fixes the meaning of every answer.

  2. Respondents answer

    Respondents answer in the browser. Finishing an anonymous survey produces a submission that holds no personal information.

  3. Import and read results

    An administrator imports the submissions. Signal recalculates results deterministically and shows how they were derived.
