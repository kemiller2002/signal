Signal

Surveys whose answers live in the link

Signal runs surveys and assessments from published, immutable templates. Each respondent's answers are carried in their own link, not stored on a server, and results are calculated the same way every time.

Open the administrator console

Answering a survey? Use the link your survey administrator sent you. Signal never asks you to sign in to answer.

Who it is for

  • Survey authors publish a template whose questions, branching, validation and scoring cannot change after publication.
  • Respondents answer from a link. The link always holds their current answers, so they can stop and continue later.
  • Administrators import completed submissions and read results with the calculation behind every figure.

How it works

  1. An author publishes a template. Its version fixes the meaning of every answer.
  2. Respondents answer in the browser. Finishing produces an anonymous submission with no personal information.
  3. An administrator imports the submissions. Signal recalculates results deterministically and shows where each number came from.
